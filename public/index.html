Capabilities

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Lumen is built around a small, opinionated set of capabilities that cover the full lifecycle of modern product work — from planning and shipping to measuring what matters.

  • Real-time insights

    Live dashboards surface what shipped, what's blocked, and what's at risk — so every team meeting starts with the same ground truth.

  • Unified planning

    Roadmaps, sprints, and backlogs live in one calm view. Replace six tools with one workspace your team actually enjoys opening.

  • Built-in automation

    Trigger deploys, handoffs, and notifications from any event. No glue scripts — just rules that read like English.

  • Time-aware reporting

    See cycle time, scope changes, and outcomes as they evolve. Spot regressions the week they happen, not the quarter after.

  • Enterprise-grade security

    SOC 2 Type II, SSO, granular roles, and full audit logs out of the box — so procurement stops being the bottleneck.

  • Calm collaboration

    Threaded updates, @mentions, and quiet-by-default notifications that respect focus time and async teammates.
