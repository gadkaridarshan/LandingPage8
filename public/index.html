Features

Everything you need to move from idea to shipped.

Lumen brings the pieces of product development into one focused workspace — so the right work always finds its way to the top.

  • Unified backlog

    Pull feedback from email, Slack, and support tickets into a single, ranked board — no more scattered spreadsheets.

  • Smart prioritization

    Lumen scores every request against impact, effort, and segment fit — so your roadmap reflects what matters most.

  • Live dashboards

    Track velocity, satisfaction, and ship dates in real time — share progress with stakeholders in a single link.

  • Enterprise-grade security

    SSO, audit logs, role-based access, and SOC 2 controls out of the box — so security review is a one-pager, not a quarter.

  • Native integrations

    First-class connections to Linear, Jira, GitHub, and Slack — your tools stay in sync without a single line of glue code.

  • Customer signal

    Reply to feedback from inside Lumen and close the loop automatically — every customer hears back, every time.
